Twenty-Two Dots

Real fixtures, played first by marbles.
Short, voiceless football videos in which twenty-two identical dots play out each real match as a physics simulation before it kicks off.

  • Tied to real fixturesEvery Short follows an actual upcoming match, and a report afterwards shows how the simulation compared with the real result.
  • Drawn, not filmedNo broadcast footage, photos, crests or kits. Each match is simulated and animated from scratch, with no narration.
  • For fun onlyA physics simulation for fun, not a forecast to bet on.
