Mola

Block apps. Take real breaks.
Mola (MO‑lah) is Turkish for "a break" — a screen time blocker that gives you a break, not a cage.

  • A shield, not a prisonChoose how serious you want to be — Gentle, Balanced, or Strict. You're in charge, every time.
  • Built to actually workThe shield holds even if you force-quit Mola or restart your phone. The block you set is the block you get.
  • Blocks on your scheduleSet recurring blocks for work hours, evenings, or bedtime — Mola shields your distractions automatically, even when it's closed.
  • Your data never leaves your phoneNo account, no sign-up. We literally can't see your screen time — everything stays on your device.
