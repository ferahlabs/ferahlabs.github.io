Privacy Policy

Aheste · Last updated: June 12, 2026

Aheste is built so that your personal data stays yours. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no developer-run database. Your routines stay on your phone and in your own private iCloud — we never see them.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Aheste — routines, steps, schedules, and your completion history — is stored on your device and, if you are signed into iCloud, synced through your private iCloud database so it survives reinstalls and moves with your devices. This data is protected by your Apple ID; we have no access to it, no copy of it, and no server of our own. If you are not signed into iCloud, everything simply stays on the device.

Anonymized usage analytics

We use TelemetryDeck to understand how the app is used in aggregate (for example, "how many users completed a routine today"). TelemetryDeck is a privacy-first service: data is anonymized before it reaches us, it cannot be used to identify you, and it is never sold or shared. Aheste does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed entirely by Apple. To manage subscription status we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt — never your name, email, or Apple ID. We do not see your payment details.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They do not involve any server.

What we never do

  • No selling or sharing of personal data — we don't have it in the first place.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, no cross-app tracking.
  • No health or medical data collection. Aheste is a productivity tool, not a medical device.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you are always in control: deleting the app removes all of it. If you have questions about this policy or your rights under GDPR, KVKK, or similar laws, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
