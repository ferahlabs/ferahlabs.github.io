Aheste

Routines that actually start themselves.
Aheste (ah‑HES‑teh) is Turkish for "unhurried" — and that's the whole idea.

  • One step at a timeThe current step is the whole screen. No lists staring back at you.
  • Know when you'll be done"Start now → done by 8:10." Gentle timers that beat time blindness, never alarm you.
  • Synced to your iCloud, not oursNo account. Your routines live in your private iCloud — invisible, automatic, never seen by us.
  • No streaks. No shame.Skipped a day? "You came back — that's the skill." Designed with ADHD brains in mind.
